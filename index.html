 MANA SHOWDOWN

Race to cast.

Charge up mana to unleash spells against your opponent. The more spells you cast the higher the damage output becomes.

  1. Build Up Mana Cycle through your deck, moving cards in alternating black/red descending order into your mana columns to charge up your mana
  2. Aces can be used as 1 or 10, using an Ace to cast a spell grants a +10 hp bonus
  3. Spells can be cast once you have the required mana. Each time you cast a spell its cost and damage will increase next use, except for healing spells which always remain 4 mana to cast.
  4. Combos allow you to stack up damage, by performing multiple attacks in a row you'll get damage modifiers
  5. Abilities allow you to expose an Ace, draw through the deck 1 card at a time for a short period or reshuffle your deck if you get stuck

Double-click an exposed card to move it to the first legal column. Use Hint for a suggested move. Undo reverses your last action.
